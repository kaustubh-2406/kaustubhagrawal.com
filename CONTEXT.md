# kaustubhagrawal.com

The personal site of one developer: who he is, what he has built, and where he has worked, for recruiters, developers and (later) blog readers.

## Language

### Career

**Role**:
One position held at one company over a date span, e.g. "Software Developer Intern at Alpha BI".
_Avoid_: job, position

**Employer**:
A company with every Role held there, e.g. AlphaBI's internship and full-time role.
_Avoid_: company entry, experience item

**Current role**:
The full-time Role with no end date.

**Resume**:
The one-page PDF of Kaustubh's career, printed from the career data and served at `/kaustubh-agrawal-resume.pdf`.
_Avoid_: CV, full CV (in the US a CV is a long academic record; research 34). Print-side code keeps its older `cv` names (`cvTitle`, `cvLine`, `cv-print`).

### Work

**Project**:
Something Kaustubh built, with a status of building, shipped or archived. Has its own page.
_Avoid_: work, portfolio item, case study

**Current project**:
The most recently started Project that is still building; it's the project shown on Home.

### Site

**Glance card**:
A side summary of key facts about a page's subject (Kaustubh on Home, a Project on its page).
_Avoid_: info card, sidebar card

**Doodle**:
The hand-lettered look with a scribbled underline, used for section headings. A **doodle note** is the hand-written aside without the underline.
_Avoid_: doodle heading, scribble

**Sidebar**:
The site-wide frame on the left: name, tagline, nav and social links. Not part of a page's content.
_Avoid_: nav bar, chrome (except in print code)
